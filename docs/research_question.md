# Research question

Can a reference-free, evidence-annotated de Bruijn graph distinguish postmortem-damage-induced paths from genuine biological variation during de novo assembly of ancient bacterial and archaeal metagenomes, improving assembly accuracy and genome recovery without collapsing low-abundance strain variation?

## Hypothesis

Damage-derived paths should be supported mainly by damage-compatible substitutions near molecule ends. Genuine variation should receive support across molecule positions, orientations, and independent fragments.

Anvaya will retain this evidence and classify competing paths as likely damage-derived, likely genuine, or unresolved. Unresolved paths will be preserved rather than corrected aggressively.
